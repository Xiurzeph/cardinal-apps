What are your typical working hours?
Our C2C execution capacity is strictly dedicated to evening scheduled maintenance windows (typically 6:00 PM to Midnight EST) and asynchronous project work. We do not take on daytime-dependent contracts.
How does billing and deployment work?
We operate solely as a 1099 Corp-to-Corp (C2C) entity. Engagements can be structured as block-hour retainers for MSPs, flat-rate asynchronous automation projects, or short-term evening contracts through technical recruiters.
Are you available for onsite work?
